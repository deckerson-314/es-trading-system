Pareto Size? Number of Pareto-optimal solutions found over generations. Generally increases over time as the GA explores the solution space. Pareto Size Interpretation: Early generations typically have few Pareto solutions. As optimization progresses, more non-dominated solutions are discovered. If the size plateaus early, the GA may have converged. If it keeps growing, the solution space is rich with trade-offs.
